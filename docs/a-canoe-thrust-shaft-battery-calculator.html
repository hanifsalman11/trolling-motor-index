Frequently Asked Questions (A Canoe)

How much trolling motor thrust do I need for A Canoe (3,500 lbs)?

Using the industry rule of 2 lbs of thrust per 100 lbs of fully loaded wet weight (3,500 lbs = 70 lbs minimum) plus a 15%–20% reserve for wind and tidal current, an 80 lb thrust (24V) motor is the ideal match.

What shaft length do I need for a 26-inch bow-to-waterline measurement?

With 26 inches from the bow mounting deck to the waterline, add 20 inches for calm lakes (46") or 24+ inches for rough open water (50"), making a 60-inch shaft the proper fit so the prop stays submerged.

How long will a 100Ah LiFePO4 battery run an 80 lb (24V) trolling motor?

At full wide-open throttle (56A), a 100Ah LiFePO4 battery runs continuously for 1.8 hours. In real-world GPS Spot-Lock and 40%–50% speed fishing (~19.6A average draw), a 100Ah LiFePO4 bank lasts 5.1 hours (1 to 2 full tournament days).
