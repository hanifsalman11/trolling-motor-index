Frequently Asked Questions (A 16 Foot Boat)

How much trolling motor thrust do I need for A 16 Foot Boat?

For a fully rigged 16-foot boat weighing up to 3,500 lbs loaded (including engine, fuel, gear, and crew), ABYC guidelines suggest 2 lbs of thrust per 100 lbs of weight, making an 80 lb thrust 24V system the ideal configuration for wind and current resistance.

What shaft length is required for A 16 Foot Boat?

With a bow-to-waterline height of 26 inches, you need at least a 60-inch shaft (26" bow height + 20" submergence + 14" motor head clearance) to prevent the prop from cavitating when pitching through rough water.

How long will a 100Ah LiFePO4 battery run on A 16 Foot Boat?

A 24V 100Ah LiFePO4 battery delivers approximately 1.78 hours of continuous run time at wide-open throttle (56A draw) and approximately 5.1 hours under typical mid-speed cruising or active GPS Spot-Lock conditions (19.6A average draw).
